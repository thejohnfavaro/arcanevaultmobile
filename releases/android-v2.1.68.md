# Arcane Vault Android v2.1.68

## Added
- Long-press life +/− to adjust five points at once.
- Long-press commander damage +/− for five-point increments.
- Long-press the panel color control for a named eight-color palette. Tap still cycles colors.
- Unit coverage for reversible per-player color choices and independent commander damage changes.

## Changed
- Responsive 2–4 player tabletop panel arrangements.
- Landscape life totals sit beside the large controls to avoid being squeezed by stacked buttons.
- Portrait layouts retain the full-sized life totals and touch-friendly controls.
- Player names, counter buttons, and accessibility descriptions are easier to read and target.
- Commander-damage and poison dialogs update their values in place, keeping controls accessible through multiple adjustments.

## Fixed
- Landscape layouts that left too little room for the life total on shorter screens.
- Commander-damage and poison dialogs that could become stale while rebuilding the table during adjustment.

## Removed
- Nothing removed. Existing tabletop games, ownership, deck library, valuation, and sync data remain unchanged.

This update affects the offline Tabletop Companion UI and its own reversible session state only.
